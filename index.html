About Parker Automotive

The local mechanic who tells it to you straight

Parker Automotive is a Christchurch workshop built on a simple idea: explain the problem in words you understand, quote it fairly, and do the job properly. No jargon, no pressure to replace things that are fine.

Whether it's the family wagon due for a warrant or a daily runabout making a new noise, you get the same honest answer and a car you can trust back on the road.
