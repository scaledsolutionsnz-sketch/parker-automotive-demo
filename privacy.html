Privacy Policy

Last updated June 2026

Parker Automotive respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle the information you share with us. This is placeholder content for the demo and should be reviewed by the business before going live.

1. What we collect

When you use our contact or feedback forms we collect the details you provide — typically your name, email, phone number, vehicle details and your message. We only collect what we need to respond to you.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange your booking.
  • To provide a quote and carry out the work you've asked about.
  • To follow up on feedback you've submitted.

3. How we store it

Form submissions are delivered to us by email. We do not sell or trade your personal information. We keep your details only as long as needed to provide our services and meet our legal obligations.

4. Third parties

Our forms are processed by FormSubmit, which passes your message to our email inbox. We don't share your information with anyone else except where required by law.

5. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and request corrections. Contact us to do so.

6. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to parkerauto89@outlook.com or by phone on 027 331 0382.
